❓ FAQ

How do I convert km/h to mph?

Multiply km/h by 0.621371. This tool handles it automatically.

What is a knot?

A knot is one nautical mile per hour (about 1.852 km/h), used in aviation and shipping.

Which unit is SI?

Meters per second (m/s) is the SI unit of speed.
