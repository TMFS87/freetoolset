What's New on FreeToolset

A running log of the tools, features, and improvements we ship. FreeToolset is a free, privacy-first toolbox — every tool runs in your browser, nothing is uploaded, and there is no account required. This page is updated as we grow.

August 2026

  • Full English site. Every page — all 145+ tools, category hubs, the blog, and the AI Studio — is now fully in English. Legacy bilingual snippets and unused tracking code were removed, and the content generators were updated so the site stays English even when pages are regenerated.
  • Freshness signal. This changelog was added so you can see what's new at a glance and trust that the site is actively maintained.

2026

  • AI Content Studio. A free AI writing assistant that generates product descriptions, blog posts, SEO meta tags, and email subject lines. It grants 5 free credits every day with no sign-up.
  • 145+ free tools across 6 categories. AI writing, calculators, unit converters, text tools, developer tools, and fun tools — all in one place, all free.
  • Local-first privacy. All computation happens in your browser. You can even keep using most tools offline, and sensitive inputs (salary, passwords, private text) never leave your device.
  • Tutorials & Guides. A growing library of practical, step-by-step guides — from JSON formatting to regular expressions to password security.
