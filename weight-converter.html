❓ FAQ

How many pounds in a kilogram?

One kilogram equals about 2.20462 pounds.

What is a stone?

A stone is a British unit of mass equal to 14 pounds (about 6.35 kg), often used for body weight.

Is mass the same as weight?

In everyday use they are treated the same; this tool converts mass units commonly called "weight".
