🔄 Reverse Text

Reverse text by characters, words or lines. Flip a string backwards or reverse word/line order instantly.

🔒 All processing happens in your browser. No data is uploaded to any server.

📋 How to Use

  1. Type or paste your text.
  2. Choose the reverse mode (characters, words or lines).
  3. The reversed text appears instantly.
  4. Copy the result with the copy button.

❓ FAQ

What is the difference between the modes?

"Characters" flips the whole string backwards; "Words" reverses word order; "Lines" reverses the order of lines.

Does it support emoji?

Character reversal is grapheme-aware for most emoji, though some complex sequences may split.

Is my text uploaded?

No. Reversal happens entirely in your browser.
