❓ FAQ

How many centimeters are in an inch?

One inch equals exactly 2.54 centimeters.

How do I convert meters to feet?

Multiply meters by 3.28084. This tool does it automatically when you pick the units.

Is the mile statute or nautical?

"Mile (mi)" is the statute mile (1609.344 m). A separate nautical mile option (1852 m) is included.
