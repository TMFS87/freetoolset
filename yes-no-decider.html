🤔 Yes or No Decider

Stuck on a simple choice? Let the Yes/No decider make it for you. Occasionally throws in a “Maybe” for the truly undecided.

🔒 All processing happens in your browser. No data is uploaded to any server.

📋 How to Use

  1. Think of your yes/no question.
  2. Click Decide.
  3. Accept the verdict (or ask again).
  4. Use only for trivial choices!

❓ FAQ

Is it really random?

Yes — each click is an independent random pick weighted mostly to Yes/No with a small chance of Maybe.

Should I use this for important decisions?

No. This is for fun and trivial choices. Real life decisions deserve real thought.

Why does it say Maybe sometimes?

A small probability returns Maybe to reflect that some questions are genuinely undecided.
