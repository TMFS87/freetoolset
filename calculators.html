Frequently Asked Questions About Calculators

Are the calculator results accurate?
Our calculators use standard math and medical formulas (such as the Mifflin-St Jeor calorie formula and the US Navy body-fat formula). Results are for reference only — for precise values, please rely on professional testing.
Do I need to register to use them?
No. All calculators work instantly — no registration, login, or app download required.
Is my input data saved?
No. All calculations run locally in your browser, and the data disappears as soon as you close the page. We never store any user input.
Can I use these calculators on my phone?
Absolutely. The responsive design ensures a good input experience on phone screens — handy for calculating discounts while shopping or BMI at the gym.
