❓ FAQ

What grade scale is used?

A=4.0, A-=3.7, B+=3.3, B=3.0, B-=2.7, C+=2.3, C=2.0, C-=1.7, D=1.0, F=0.0 — a common US 4.0 scale.

What is a weighted GPA?

Each course grade is multiplied by its credit hours; the sum is divided by total credits, so higher-credit courses count more.

Can I remove a course?

Yes, click the ✕ on any row to remove it and the GPA recalculates.
