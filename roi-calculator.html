❓ FAQ

How is ROI calculated?

ROI = (final value − initial cost) / initial cost × 100, expressed as a percentage.

What is annualized return?

It converts total ROI into an equivalent yearly rate using (final/initial)^(1/years) − 1, so investments of different lengths can be compared.

Can ROI be negative?

Yes. A negative ROI means the investment lost value relative to its cost.
