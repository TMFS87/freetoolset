❓ FAQ

What is the difference between torque and power?

Torque is rotational force (N·m); power is how fast that force does work (kW or hp). Power = torque × angular speed.

Is N·m the same as kgf·m?

No. 1 kgf·m ≈ 9.81 N·m because it uses the force of 1 kg under gravity. They are close but not equal.

When do I use lbf·ft?

US automotive and engineering specs commonly quote engine and fastener torque in pound-force feet.

📖 Deep Dive: Torque Converter

What is torque

Torque (moment of force) measures the rotational effect of force times lever arm, with the international unit newton-meter (N m).

Common units

  • N m: international standard.
  • lbf ft: imperial, common in US automotive and machinery.
  • kgf m: kilogram-force meter, old system, 1 kgf m is about 9.80665 N m.
  • N cm: small torque, 1 N m = 100 N cm.

    • Conversion note

    All units are linearly converted to N m first, then to the target unit.
