📋 How to Use

  1. Enter the total bill amount.

  3. Set how many people are splitting.
  4. See the tip, grand total and amount per person.

❓ FAQ

How much should I tip?

In the US, 15–20% is customary for good service. Tipping norms vary widely by country, so check local customs.

Does it split the tip too?

Yes. The per-person amount includes each person’s share of both the bill and the tip.

Can I tip on the pre-tax amount?

Enter the pre-tax subtotal in the bill field if you prefer to tip before tax.
