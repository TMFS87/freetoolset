↵ Remove Line Breaks

Remove line breaks and paragraph breaks from text. Join broken lines into a single paragraph, with an option to keep paragraph spacing.

🔒 All processing happens in your browser. No data is uploaded to any server.

📋 How to Use

  1. Paste text with unwanted line breaks.
  2. Choose whether to keep paragraph breaks.
  3. Pick what to replace line breaks with (space or nothing).
  4. Copy the cleaned single-block text.

❓ FAQ

Will it keep paragraphs?

If you enable "Keep paragraph breaks", blank-line separated paragraphs are preserved while single line breaks inside them are removed.

What replaces the line break?

You can replace each removed break with a space (recommended) or with nothing.

Good for pasted PDF text?

Yes — it fixes text that wraps awkwardly after copying from PDFs or emails.
