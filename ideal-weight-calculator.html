❓ FAQ

Why do the four formulas disagree?

They were derived from different populations and eras. Treat the spread between them as a reasonable range rather than looking for one exact number.

Is ideal weight the same as healthy weight?

No. The healthy BMI range (18.5–24.9) is wider and more forgiving. Ideal weight formulas give a single point estimate originally designed for medication dosing.

📖 Deep Dive: Ideal Weight Calculator

Where ideal weight comes from

The idea of ideal weight first appeared in the 1970s. The Devine formula was originally designed to calculate drug dosages, then became widely used for health assessment. Later scholars such as Robinson, Miller, and Hamwi proposed their own revised versions.

Differences between the four formulas

Take a 170cm man as an example: Devine about 65.9kg, Robinson about 64.9kg, Miller about 64.5kg, Hamwi about 68.0kg. The differences come from their base weights and per-inch increments, usually 2-4kg apart.

A more practical reference: the BMI range

The WHO recommends adults keep BMI between 18.5 and 24.9. Converted to weight that is a range rather than a single point; for 170cm a healthy weight is roughly 53.5-72.0kg. This range better accommodates different body types and muscle mass.

Limitations

  • Formulas consider only height and sex, ignoring muscle mass and frame size.
  • Athletes with high muscle density are often labeled overweight but are actually healthy.
  • Not suitable for children, pregnant women, or the elderly.
  • Results are for reference only and do not constitute medical advice.
