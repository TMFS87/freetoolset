❓ FAQ

Is this a strict medical rule?

No. It is a general guideline based on weight and lifestyle. Individual needs vary with health, diet and environment.

Does coffee or tea count?

Roughly yes, though caffeinated drinks have a mild diuretic effect. Most of your intake should be plain water.

Why does activity increase needs?

Exercise and heat increase fluid loss through sweat, so the body needs more water to stay balanced.

📖 Deep Dive: Daily Water Intake Calculator

Why a water reminder

Many people are chronically under-hydrated, and thirst lags behind the body's actual need. A weight-based estimate gives an actionable daily target.

How it works

A common baseline is weight in kg times 35ml, then add about 500ml each for activity level and hot climate, giving the daily recommended intake.

A quick example

Weight 70kg, moderate activity, not hot: 70 times 35 equals 2450ml, plus 500ml for activity - about 3 liters per day.
