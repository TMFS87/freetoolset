❓ FAQ

Is a food calorie the same as a calorie?

No. The "calorie" on nutrition labels is actually a kilocalorie (kcal) — 1000 small calories. A 500 kcal meal is 500,000 calories in physics terms.

How many joules in 1 kWh?

1 kilowatt-hour = 3,600,000 joules (3.6 MJ). That is one kilowatt sustained for one hour.

📖 Deep Dive: Energy Converter

The international unit of energy

The joule (J) is the international standard unit of energy, defined as the work done by one newton of force acting over one meter. Every other energy unit can be converted into joules.

Common units by field

  • Food nutrition: kilocalorie (kcal) and kilojoule (kJ), 1 kcal = 4.184 kJ.
  • Electricity: kilowatt-hour (kWh), 1 kWh = 3.6 MJ.
  • HVAC: British thermal unit (BTU), 1 BTU is about 1055 J.
  • Microphysics: electronvolt (eV), 1 eV is about 1.602 times 10 to the power of -19 J.

    • A practical conversion

    A food labeled 1500 kJ per 100g converts to kcal: 1500 divided by 4.184 is about 358 kcal. Chinese nutrition labels usually show kilojoules directly, so people used to kcal need to divide by 4.184.

    What air-conditioner BTU means

    An air conditioner rated 12000 BTU/h is about 3.5 kW, commonly called a 1.5-horsepower unit. BTU is energy per hour, so keep energy and power distinct when converting.
