❓ FAQ

How do I calculate percentage change?

Percentage change = (new − old) / old × 100. A positive result is an increase; a negative result is a decrease.

What does "X is what percent of Y" mean?

It tells you what proportion X represents of Y, calculated as X / Y × 100.

Are decimals supported?

Yes, you can enter decimal values in any field and results are rounded to two decimals.

百分比怎么算？

基本公式：部分 ÷ 整体 × 100%。例如 20 占 80 就是 25%。

增长百分比怎么算？

用 (新值 − 旧值) ÷ 旧值 × 100%。结果为正表示上涨，为负表示下降。
