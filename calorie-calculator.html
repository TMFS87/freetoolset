❓ FAQ

What is TDEE?

Total Daily Energy Expenditure is the number of calories you burn per day including activity. Eating at TDEE maintains weight.

How do I lose weight?

A deficit of about 500 calories per day typically leads to roughly 0.45 kg (1 lb) of loss per week. Never go below your BMR without medical advice.

How accurate is this?

The Mifflin-St Jeor equation is one of the most accurate estimates, but individual metabolism varies. Use it as a starting point.
