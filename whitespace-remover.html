  4. Copy the tidy result.

❓ FAQ

What does "collapse spaces" do?

It replaces runs of multiple spaces/tabs with a single space, keeping words readable.

Will it remove empty lines?

Yes, when "Remove blank lines" is enabled, all empty or whitespace-only lines are deleted.

Can I remove all spaces?

Yes. "Remove all spaces" strips every space character, useful for compacting strings.
