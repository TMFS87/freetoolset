❓ FAQ

Is the result truly random?

It uses the browser's pseudo-random generator, which is unpredictable enough for games and decisions.

Can I flip many times quickly?

Yes. Each click is one flip and the counter updates, so you can build a tally fast.

Does it favor either side?

Over many flips the counts tend toward 50/50, just like a real coin.

📖 Deep Dive: Coin Flip

Why flip a coin

Faced with a two-way, evenly matched choice, a coin flip breaks the deadlock fast and hands the decision anxiety over to chance.

How it works

The tool uses Math.random() to generate a 0-1 number; below 0.5 is heads, otherwise tails, and it tallies heads and tails counts.

A quick example

Two people argue over who goes first; flip three times to decide order, and the counter shows the current heads or tails split - an open and transparent process.
