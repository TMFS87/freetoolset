🔒 All processing happens in your browser. No data is uploaded to any server.

📋 How to Use

  1. Click "Flip".
  2. See the result: Heads or Tails.
  3. Flip again as many times as you like.
  4. Watch the running heads/tails tally.

❓ FAQ

Is the result truly random?

It uses the browser's pseudo-random generator, which is unpredictable enough for games and decisions.

Can I flip many times quickly?

Yes. Each click is one flip and the counter updates, so you can build a tally fast.

Does it favor either side?

Over many flips the counts tend toward 50/50, just like a real coin.

📖 深入了解 Coin Flip

为什么需要抛硬币

面对二选一、势均力敌的情况，抛硬币能快速打破僵局，也把「选择焦虑」交给运气。

背后的原理

工具用 Math.random() 生成 0–1 的数，小于 0.5 判正面、否则反面，并累计正反面次数。

一个例子

两个人争着先选，连翻三次定先后，计数器显示当前正反面分布，过程公开透明。
