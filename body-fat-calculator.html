❓ FAQ

How accurate is the Navy method?

It correlates well with more precise methods for most people, but athletes with unusual muscle distribution may see larger error. Treat it as an estimate.

Why do women enter hip circumference?

The female formula accounts for hip fat distribution, which the male formula does not need.

What units should I use?

Use centimeters for all measurements to match the formula constants.

📖 Deep Dive: Body Fat Calculator

Why estimate body fat

People with the same weight can have very different body fat; body fat percent reflects body composition better than BMI. The circumference method needs no equipment, ideal for home self-testing.

How it works

The Navy method plugs height, waist, and neck (plus hip for women) into an empirical formula to estimate body fat percentage; the formula uses separate constants for men and women.

A quick example

Male, height 175cm, waist 85cm, neck 38cm - the formula gives about 20% body fat, within the healthy range.
