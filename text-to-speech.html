🔊 Text to Speech

Convert text to natural-sounding speech in your browser. Choose a voice, adjust speed and pitch, then play, pause or stop — no signup.

🔒 All processing happens in your browser. No data is uploaded to any server.

📋 How to Use

  1. Type or paste the text to read.
  2. Select a voice from your system.
  3. Adjust the speed and pitch sliders.
  4. Press Play to listen; use Pause or Stop anytime.

❓ FAQ

Which voices are available?

The tool uses your device and browser voices, so the list depends on your operating system. More voices may appear on desktop browsers.

Is audio uploaded anywhere?

No. Speech is synthesized locally by your browser; nothing is sent to a server.

Can I download the audio?

Browser speech synthesis plays audio live and does not provide a downloadable file in most browsers.
