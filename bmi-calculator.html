❓ FAQ

What is a healthy BMI range?

For most adults a BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 is considered healthy. Below 18.5 is underweight, 25–29.9 is overweight, and 30+ is obese.

Is BMI accurate for athletes?

BMI does not distinguish muscle from fat, so very muscular people may show a high BMI while being healthy. Use it as a general guide, not a diagnosis.

Does this work for children?

This calculator uses the adult formula. Children and teens should use age- and sex-specific BMI percentiles from a pediatric chart.
