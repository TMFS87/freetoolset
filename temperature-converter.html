❓ FAQ

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Multiply by 9/5 and add 32: °F = °C × 9/5 + 32. This tool does it automatically.

What is Kelvin used for?

Kelvin is the SI base unit of temperature used in science; 0 K is absolute zero, equal to −273.15 °C.

Can temperatures be negative?

Celsius and Fahrenheit can be negative, but Kelvin cannot go below 0.
