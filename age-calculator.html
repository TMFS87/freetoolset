🎂 Age Calculator

Calculate your exact age in years, months and days from your date of birth. See total days lived and days until your next birthday.

Select your date of birth to calculate your age.
🔒 All processing happens in your browser. No data is uploaded to any server.

📋 How to Use

  1. Pick your date of birth.
  2. Optionally change the "age at" date (defaults to today).
  3. See your exact age broken down by years, months and days.
  4. Check total days lived and the countdown to your next birthday.

❓ FAQ

How is age calculated?

We compute the difference between your birth date and the target date, accounting for varying month lengths and leap years, then express it as years, months and days.

Can I calculate age on a future date?

Yes. Change the "Age at date" field to any date to see how old you (or someone) will be then.

Is my birth date stored anywhere?

No. Everything is calculated locally in your browser and nothing is uploaded.
