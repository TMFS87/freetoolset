❓ FAQ

Will these work everywhere?

They are real Unicode characters, so they work in most apps that support Unicode (Instagram, X, Discord, etc.). A few platforms may not render every style.

Is this a real font?

No — it maps letters to styled Unicode symbols, so no font install is needed and it copies as text.

Why do some characters look plain?

Only letters and digits have styled equivalents; punctuation stays as-is.
