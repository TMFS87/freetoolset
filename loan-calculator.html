❓ FAQ

What is EMI?

EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) is the fixed amount you pay each month, covering both principal and interest, so the loan is fully repaid by the end of the term.

How is monthly payment calculated?

We use the standard amortization formula: P × r × (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n − 1), where r is the monthly rate and n is the number of months.

Does this include fees or insurance?

No. This is a principal-plus-interest estimate. Actual costs may include processing fees, insurance or taxes.
