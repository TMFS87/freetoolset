📋 How to Use

  4. Click Calculate to see your plan.

❓ FAQ

Does it account for interest?

Yes. Contributions compound monthly at the annual rate you enter. Set the rate to 0 for a plain cash savings plan.

Should I include inflation?

This tool works in nominal terms. For a rough real-terms view, subtract expected inflation from your interest rate before entering it.

❓ More Questions

Does the calculation include interest?

Yes. Money deposited each month compounds monthly at the annual rate you enter. If you just leave it in a no-interest account, set the rate to 0.

Should I account for inflation?

This tool uses nominal amounts. To see real purchasing power, enter the difference between the annual rate and your expected inflation rate for an approximate real-return view.

📖 Deep Dive: Savings Goal Calculator

Two common questions

Saving plans usually come down to two questions: how much do I need to save each month to reach the goal on time, and at my current pace when will I get there. This tool supports both modes.

The formula behind it

Regular deposits with compounding use the future-value-of-an-annuity formula: FV = PV times (1+r)^n + PMT times (((1+r)^n - 1) divided by r), where r is the monthly rate and n is the number of months. Solving for PMT gives the monthly amount to save.

An example

Goal 300000 down payment, current savings 50000, annual rate 3%, want to reach it in 5 years. The formula gives about 3835 per month; without interest you would need about 4167 - interest saves you about 20000 of principal.

Ways to improve your success rate

  • Set up auto-transfer: move savings out on payday.
  • Put bonuses and tax refunds straight toward the goal.
  • Pay down high-interest debt first: clearing a 15% credit card equals a 15% risk-free return.
