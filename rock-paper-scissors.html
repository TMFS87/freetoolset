✊ Rock Paper Scissors

Play Rock Paper Scissors against a randomly choosing computer. Tracks your win/loss/tie score.

🔒 All processing happens in your browser. No data is uploaded to any server.

📋 How to Use

  1. Click Rock, Paper or Scissors.
  2. See the computer’s move and result.
  3. Watch your score update.
  4. Play best of luck!

❓ FAQ

Is the computer fair?

It picks uniformly at random each round, so over many games your win rate should approach one third.

Does it learn my pattern?

No — it is purely random and has no memory between rounds.

Can I reset the score?

Refresh the page to reset the scoreboard.

❓ 常见问题（中文）

电脑公平吗？

每回合均匀随机出招，长期看你的胜率会趋近于三分之一。

它会学我的套路吗？

不会，完全随机且各回合之间无记忆。
