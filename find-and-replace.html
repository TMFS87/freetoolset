🔍 Find and Replace Text

Find and replace text online with support for case-insensitive matching and regular expressions. See how many replacements were made.

🔒 All processing happens in your browser. No data is uploaded to any server.

📋 How to Use

  1. Paste your text in the input box.
  2. Enter the text (or regex) to find.
  3. Enter the replacement text.
  4. Toggle options and see the result with a replacement count.

❓ FAQ

Does it support regular expressions?

Yes. Enable the "Regex" option to use patterns like \\d+ or capture groups such as $1.

Is it case sensitive?

By default yes; enable "Ignore case" for case-insensitive matching.

Are all matches replaced?

Yes, every match is replaced and the total count is shown.

查找替换工具需要联网吗？

不需要。所有处理都在你的浏览器本地完成，数据不上传服务器，离线也能用。

查找替换工具支持大文件或批量吗？

在浏览器性能范围内支持较大内容处理；具体上限取决于你的设备内存。
