❓ FAQ

What is the largest number supported?

Standard Roman numerals go up to 3,999. We extend to millions using overline notation for thousands.

Is zero supported?

Romans had no numeral for zero, so 0 and negative numbers are not converted.

Is input case sensitive?

No. Roman numerals are accepted in upper or lower case.
