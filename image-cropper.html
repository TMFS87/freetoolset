✂️ Image Cropper

Crop an image to a chosen aspect ratio (1:1, 4:3, 16:9 and more) or free size. All processing stays in your browser.

🔒 All processing happens in your browser. No data is uploaded to any server.

📋 How to Use

  1. Choose an image.
  2. Pick an aspect ratio (or Original).
  3. Click Crop to cut the centered region.
  4. Download the result.

❓ FAQ

Does it crop from the center?

Yes — the largest centered rectangle of the chosen ratio is used, so nothing important at the edges is lost unexpectedly.

Can I crop a free rectangle?

This tool uses preset ratios for reliability. For pixel-precise cropping, choose Original (no change) and use an editor.

Is my photo uploaded?

No — cropping is done locally with Canvas.

❓ 常见问题（中文）

是从中心裁剪吗？

是的。工具会取所选比例下最大的居中矩形，避免意外裁掉中心内容。

能自由拖拽裁剪吗？

为保证可靠，本工具采用预设比例。如需像素级自由裁剪可选 Original（不裁剪）再用其他编辑器。

📖 深入了解 Image Cropper

工作原理

按目标比例计算“最大居中矩形”，用 Canvas 把该区域绘制到新画布并导出。

比例说明

  • 1:1 正方形（头像）
  • 4:3 / 3:4 通用照片
  • 16:9 / 9:16 横/竖封面
  • Original 不裁剪
