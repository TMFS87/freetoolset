🔁 Text Repeater

Repeat any text or word multiple times with a custom separator. Great for testing, filler content and social posts.

🔒 All processing happens in your browser. No data is uploaded to any server.

📋 How to Use

  1. Enter the text you want to repeat.
  2. Set how many times to repeat it.
  3. Choose a separator between repeats.
  4. Copy the generated output.

❓ FAQ

Is there a repeat limit?

You can repeat up to 10,000 times; very large outputs may be slow to display.

What separators are available?

New line, space, comma, or none — pick what suits your use.

Can I repeat multiple lines?

Yes, the entire input block is repeated as one unit.
