❓ FAQ

What is included in the payment?

This estimate covers principal and interest only. Property tax, home insurance and HOA fees are not included.

How much should my down payment be?

A common guideline is 20% of the home price to avoid mortgage insurance, but requirements vary by lender and country.

Can I change the term?

Yes. Try 15, 20 or 30 years to see how the term affects your monthly payment and total interest.
