❓ FAQ

What is entropy?

Entropy (in bits) measures randomness. It equals length × log2(pool size). More bits = harder to guess. 60+ bits is considered strong; 128+ is excellent.

How is crack time estimated?

We assume an offline attacker guessing 10 billion passwords per second (common for hashed dumps). Time = 2^entropy ÷ 2 ÷ guess-rate. It's an estimate, not a guarantee.

Is my password saved?

No. Analysis happens entirely in your browser tab. Nothing is sent anywhere or stored. Close the tab and it's gone.

密码强度检测需要联网吗？

不需要。所有处理都在你的浏览器本地完成，数据不上传服务器，离线也能用。

密码强度检测支持大文件或批量吗？

在浏览器性能范围内支持较大内容处理；具体上限取决于你的设备内存。
