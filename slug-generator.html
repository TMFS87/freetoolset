🔗 URL Slug Generator

Turn any title into a clean, SEO-friendly URL slug. Lowercases text, strips accents and replaces spaces with hyphens.

🔒 All processing happens in your browser. No data is uploaded to any server.

📋 How to Use

  1. Type or paste your title or heading.
  2. Choose a separator (hyphen or underscore).
  3. The URL-safe slug is generated instantly.
  4. Copy it into your CMS or code.

❓ FAQ

Why use hyphens in URLs?

Search engines treat hyphens as word separators, so hyphenated slugs are more readable and SEO-friendly than underscores or spaces.

Does it remove accents?

Yes. Accented characters like é or ü are converted to their base letters (e, u).

What about special characters?

Punctuation and symbols are removed, leaving only letters, numbers and the separator.
