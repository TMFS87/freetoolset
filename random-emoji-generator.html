❓ FAQ

Where do the emojis come from?

From a curated list of common emojis stored in the page — no download or network needed.

Can I copy the emoji?

Yes — click the big emoji to copy it to your clipboard.

Will it work everywhere?

Emoji rendering depends on your device OS and font, but the characters themselves are standard Unicode.
