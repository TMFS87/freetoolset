🔢 Character Counter

Count characters (with and without spaces), words, lines and paragraphs in your text in real time. Ideal for essays, tweets and metadata limits.

🔒 All processing happens in your browser. No data is uploaded to any server.

📋 How to Use

  1. Type or paste your text.
  2. Watch the counts update live.
  3. Use the numbers to stay within limits.
  4. No button needed — it is instant.

❓ FAQ

What counts as a word?

A word is a run of characters separated by spaces or line breaks. This is a simple heuristic, good for estimates.

Does it count spaces?

Yes — there are two figures: total characters (including spaces) and characters excluding spaces, so you can pick whichever you need.

Is there a limit on input size?

It handles typical pastes fine; extremely large texts may feel slower but still work since everything runs locally.

📖 Deep Dive: Character Counter

Why it is useful

Twitter, headlines and meta descriptions all have character limits; live counting helps you stay within them.

The metrics

  • Characters (with spaces): total count of every character
  • Characters (without spaces): length after removing spaces
  • Words: segments separated by whitespace
  • Lines: newlines + 1
