❓ FAQ

What is compound interest?

Compound interest is interest earned on both your original principal and previously accumulated interest, so growth accelerates over time.

How does compounding frequency matter?

More frequent compounding (e.g. monthly vs annually) produces slightly higher returns for the same rate.

Are contributions added before or after interest?

This calculator adds each monthly contribution and compounds the balance, giving a close estimate of end value.
