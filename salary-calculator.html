❓ FAQ

How is annual salary from hourly calculated?

Annual = hourly × hours per week × 52. Monthly is the annual figure divided by 12.

Is this gross or net pay?

These are gross (pre-tax) figures. Taxes and deductions vary by location and are not included.

Can I change working hours?

Yes. Adjust hours per week and days per week to match your schedule for accurate conversions.
