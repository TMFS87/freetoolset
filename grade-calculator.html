❓ FAQ

Do the weights need to sum to 100?

Not strictly — the calculator normalizes by total weight, so 100% is convenient but any consistent values work.

Can I mix score scales?

Keep all scores on the same scale (e.g. 0–100) so the weighted average is meaningful.

Does it compute GPA?

It computes the weighted percentage. Convert that to GPA using your school's scale separately.

📖 深入了解 Weighted Grade Calculator

为什么需要加权成绩计算

课程成绩由多项按不同权重组成，手算加权均值容易出错。动态表格能精确匹配大纲。

背后的原理

最终成绩 = Σ(各项分数 × 权重) ÷ Σ权重。权重归一化后乘以分数再求和，得到综合百分比。

一个例子

平时 30%（得 90）、期中 30%（得 80）、期末 40%（得 70），加权 = (90×30+80×30+70×40)÷100 = 79 分。
