🔁 Simplified-Traditional Chinese Converter

Convert text between Simplified and Traditional Chinese instantly. Useful for reaching both mainland and Taiwan/HK audiences without switching apps.

Enter Chinese text and choose a direction.
🔒 All processing happens in your browser. No data is uploaded to any server.

📋 How to Use

  1. Paste Chinese text into the box.
  2. Click "Simplified to Traditional" to convert to Traditional, or "Traditional to Simplified" for Simplified.
  3. Copy the converted text.
  4. Note: conversion is based on a common-character table.

❓ FAQ

Is the conversion 100% accurate?

It uses a built-in common-character mapping table, so it covers the vast majority of everyday characters. A few rare or context-dependent characters may need manual correction.

Does it handle one-to-many mappings?

Some Traditional characters map to multiple Simplified ones (e.g. one Traditional character mapping to several Simplified forms). The table uses the most common correspondence; verify important text.

Is it private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser.

📖 Deep Dive: Simplified-Traditional Chinese Converter

Why convert between Simplified and Traditional

The Chinese internet splits into two big circles - Simplified and Traditional. Presenting the same content in both scripts reaches a wider audience. Automatic conversion is far more efficient than manual re-typing.

How it works

The tool has a built-in common-character mapping table (a few hundred high-frequency characters) and replaces each input character by looking it up. Simplified to Traditional and Traditional to Simplified use the same two-way mapping.

A quick example

Paste a Simplified article, click convert to Traditional, and in a few seconds you get the Traditional version, ready to publish on platforms aimed at Hong Kong and Taiwan readers.
