🔁 Simplified-Traditional Chinese Converter

Convert text between Simplified and Traditional Chinese instantly. Useful for reaching both mainland and Taiwan/HK audiences without switching apps.

输入中文后选择转换方向。
🔒 All processing happens in your browser. No data is uploaded to any server.

📋 How to Use

  1. Paste Chinese text into the box.
  2. Click "简→繁" to convert to Traditional, or "繁→简" for Simplified.
  3. Copy the converted text.
  4. Note: conversion is based on a common-character table.

❓ FAQ

Is the conversion 100% accurate?

It uses a built-in common-character mapping table, so it covers the vast majority of everyday characters. A few rare or context-dependent characters may need manual correction.

Does it handle one-to-many mappings?

Some Traditional characters map to multiple Simplified ones (e.g. 後/后). The table uses the most common correspondence; verify important text.

Is it private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser.

❓ 常见问题（中文）

转换完全准确吗？

基于内置常用字对照表，能覆盖绝大多数日常用字，少数生僻或一词多义的字符可能需要手动校对。

能处理一繁对多简吗？

部分繁体字对应多个简体（如「後/后」），表内取最常见对应，重要文本请人工核对。

为什么需要简繁转换

中文互联网分为简体与繁体两大圈层，同一内容用不同字形能覆盖更广读者。自动转换比人工重排高效得多。

背后的原理

工具内置一份简繁常用字对照表（约数百个高频字），对输入文本逐字查表替换。简转繁与繁转简使用同一张双向映射。

一个例子

把一篇简体公众号文章粘进来，点「简→繁」，几秒得到繁体版，可直接发到面向港台读者的平台。
