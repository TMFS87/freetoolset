The Complete Unit Conversion Guide: Length, Weight, Temperature & Data

Unit conversion comes up constantly when studying, renovating, cooking, or even checking the weather. Different countries use different systems, and memorizing conversion factors is unrealistic — letting an online tool do it is the easiest path.

Metric vs Imperial: which to use

China and Europe use metric (meters, kilograms, Celsius); the US commonly uses imperial (feet, pounds, Fahrenheit). Switching units in real time avoids plenty of errors when shopping abroad, reading overseas recipes, or reading technical docs.

Often-overlooked conversions

  • Data storage: is 1 KB 1,000 or 1,024 bytes? Decimal vs binary differ a lot
  • Area: square meters and square feet are often mixed up in real estate
  • Volume: kitchen cups/tablespoons vs milliliters need a reference
  • Temperature: the relationship between Celsius, Fahrenheit, and Kelvin
