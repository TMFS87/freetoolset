Monthly vs Hourly Pay: The Math When Switching Jobs

Different jobs quote pay differently — some annual, some hourly. Normalize to the same unit to compare real income.

How to convert

  • Annual ÷ 12 = monthly
  • Monthly ÷ 21.75 ≈ daily (monthly paid days)
  • Daily ÷ 8 ≈ hourly

Don't miss the hidden items

Social insurance, subsidies, and overtime intensity all affect take-home pay. Low hourly but little overtime may beat high hourly.

💡 With multiple offers, use a salary converter to normalize them, then weigh benefits comprehensively.
