8 Everyday Uses of Percentages

Percentages standardize 'part vs whole' so things are easy to compare. Three common types: a percentage of a number, A as a percentage of B, and percent change.

High-frequency scenarios

  • Shopping: how much a 30% discount saves
  • Grades: 85 points as a share of the total
  • Growth: monthly active users rising from 1,000 to 1,300 is +30%
  • Tips: a 200 bill plus 15% tip

The percent-change trap

Going 100→120 is +20%, but falling 120→100 is −16.7% because the base changed. Ups and downs aren't symmetric — watch the reference point.

💡 When unsure 'what percent of what,' use the percentage calculator's mode selector to avoid flipping it.
