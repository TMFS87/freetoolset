The Complete Unix Timestamp Guide: Seconds, Milliseconds & Dates

A Unix timestamp is the number of seconds since 1970-01-01, used by almost every system to record time. Understand it and time-zone and log issues become easy.

Seconds or milliseconds

Many systems use seconds (10 digits), while JavaScript's Date.now() returns milliseconds (13 digits). 1000× apart — confusing them shows the time as 1970.

UTC vs local time

  • UTC is Coordinated Universal Time, independent of time zone
  • Local time = UTC ± zone offset
  • Store in UTC, convert only when displaying

Convert online
