Celsius & Fahrenheit: Formulas and Quick Estimates

F to C: ℃ = (℉ − 32) × 5/9; C to F: ℉ = ℃ × 9/5 + 32. Water freezes at 0℃/32℉ and boils at 100℃/212℉.

Three scales

  • Celsius: everyday and scientific use
  • Fahrenheit: US weather and cooking
  • Kelvin: starts at absolute zero, used in physics/chemistry

Quick-trick estimates

Doubling Celsius and adding 30 ≈ Fahrenheit (e.g., 20℃→70℉, actual 68℉) — good for estimates.
