Discounts & Tips: Don't Miscalculate When Shopping or Dining

Discount calculates 'how much saved'; tip calculates 'how much extra' — one subtraction, one addition, but both rely on percentage basics.

Reading discounts

  • Original × (1 − discount%) = post-discount price
  • Stacked discounts aren't simple addition — multiply sequentially
  • Tell whether it's 'minus a fixed amount' or 'percent off'
