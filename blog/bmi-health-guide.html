How to Calculate BMI Accurately: Ranges, Myths & Examples

BMI (Body Mass Index) = weight(kg) ÷ height(m)². For example, at 1.7m and 65kg, BMI = 65 ÷ 2.89 ≈ 22.5, which falls in the healthy range.

Health ranges

  • Below 18.5: underweight
  • 18.5–24.9: normal
  • 25–29.9: overweight
  • 30 and above: obese

Common myths

  • Muscular people score higher on BMI but aren't necessarily unhealthy
  • BMI doesn't distinguish fat distribution and can't replace body-fat %
  • Children and pregnant women need specialized standards
💡 BMI is only a rough screen — combine it with waistline and body-fat % for a fuller picture.
