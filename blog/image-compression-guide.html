Lossless-Looking Image Compression: Practical Ways to Speed Up Pages

Images are often the largest resource on a page. Reasonable compression noticeably speeds up loading with barely visible difference — directly affecting bounce rate and SEO.

Lossy vs lossless

  • Lossy (JPEG/WebP) is smaller — good for photos
  • Lossless (PNG) keeps detail — good for icons/screenshots
  • WebP usually beats JPEG/PNG

Compression in practice

Use FreeToolset's image compressor to compress and resize right in the browser — no server upload, protecting privacy with instant output.

A few tips

  • Prefer WebP format
  • Export at display size — don't upload originals
  • Quality 75–85 is usually enough
💡 Keep above-the-fold images under 100KB for a clear mobile experience boost.
