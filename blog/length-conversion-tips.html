Length Conversion Tips: Meters, Feet & Inches

1 inch = 2.54 cm, 1 foot = 30.48 cm, 1 mile ≈ 1.609 km. Remember these and everyday estimates are covered.

Common uses

  • Overseas furniture: inches to cm
  • Renovation measuring: meters vs feet
  • Travel road signs: miles to km

Quick mental math

cm to inches ≈ divide by 2.5; feet to meters ≈ multiply by 0.3. Use the converter for exact values.
