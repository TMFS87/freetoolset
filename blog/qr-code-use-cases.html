Common Uses & Best Practices for QR Code Generation

A QR code turns information into one image — scan with a phone to read it. It goes far beyond payments and is a low-cost, efficient connection entry point.

What a QR code can hold

  • URL links
  • Plain text / WiFi password
  • Contact (vCard)
  • SMS / phone actions

8 practical scenarios

  • Business-card QR — scan to save contact
  • Event posters — drive follows to an account
  • Product packaging — jump to instructions
  • Meeting materials — quick WiFi connect
  • E-commerce parcels — track logistics
  • Offline ads — drive to a landing page
  • E-tickets — scan to redeem
  • Teaching materials — scan to get resources
