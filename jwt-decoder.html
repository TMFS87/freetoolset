🔐 JWT Decoder

Decode a JSON Web Token (JWT) to inspect its header and payload. Views claims like exp and iat locally without sending the token anywhere.

🔒 All processing happens in your browser. No data is uploaded to any server.

📋 How to Use

  1. Paste your JWT into the input box.
  2. The header and payload are decoded instantly.
  3. Review the claims and expiry time.
  4. Nothing is uploaded — it is safe to inspect.

❓ FAQ

Does it verify the signature?

No. This tool only decodes the token for inspection. Signature verification requires your secret/key and should be done server-side.

Is my token sent anywhere?

No. Decoding is fully local in your browser, so it is safe for sensitive tokens.

What do exp and iat mean?

exp is the expiration time and iat is the issued-at time, both as Unix timestamps; we show them in readable form.
