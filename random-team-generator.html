👥 Random Team Generator

Split a list of people into balanced random teams or groups. Ideal for classrooms, workshops, games, and fair project assignments.

Add names and set team count.
🔒 All processing happens in your browser. No data is uploaded to any server.

📋 How to Use

  1. Paste names, one per line.
  2. Enter the number of teams.
  3. Click "Generate".
  4. See balanced random groups.

❓ FAQ

Are the groups balanced?

Yes. Names are shuffled first, then distributed round-robin so group sizes differ by at most one person.

Can I regenerate different teams?

Yes. Each click reshuffles, so you can draw new teams until you like the mix.

What if the count does not divide evenly?

Extra people are spread one per group, keeping sizes as even as possible.

📖 Deep Dive: Random Team Generator

Why a random team generator

Manual grouping easily leads to cliques sticking together or uneven sizes. Random, even splits are both fair and time-saving.

How it works

Shuffle the list first, then assign round-robin to each group, guaranteeing a size difference of at most one and random membership per group.

A quick example

Split 24 people into 4 groups: paste them, set 4, click generate, and you get 4 groups of 6 with randomly distributed members.
