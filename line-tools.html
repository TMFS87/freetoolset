❓ FAQ

Does Dedupe keep order?

Yes. "Dedupe" removes later duplicate lines while preserving the original order of first appearances.

What does Trim do?

Trim removes leading and trailing whitespace from each line (spaces and tabs) without deleting the line itself.

Is sorting case-sensitive?

Sorting uses locale-aware comparison, so "Apple" and "banana" are ordered alphabetically regardless of case.
