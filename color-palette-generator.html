🌈 Color Palette Generator

Generate a harmonious color palette from a base color — tints, shades, complementary and analogous colors, with copy-ready hex codes.

🔒 All processing happens in your browser. No data is uploaded to any server.

📋 How to Use

  1. Pick or enter a base color.
  2. Click Generate.
  3. See the palette of tints, shades and harmonies.
  4. Click any swatch to copy its hex.

❓ FAQ

What harmonies does it produce?

It shows lighter tints and darker shades of your base, plus the complementary (opposite) color and two analogous neighbors on the color wheel.

Are the colors accessible together?

Not automatically — pair them with the Color Contrast Checker if you need text/background combinations to meet WCAG.

Can I use these in CSS?

Yes — every swatch shows a hex code you can paste straight into CSS or design tools.

❓ More Questions

Which harmonized colors are generated?

It shows the base color's tint (lighter) and shade (darker), plus the complement (opposite on the wheel) and two analogous colors.

Are these pairings readable?

Not guaranteed. For text/background combos that meet accessibility standards, use the color contrast checker together.

📖 Deep Dive: Color Palette Generator

Color basics

To generate harmonized colors from a base, common approaches are:

  • Tint: mix with white to lighten.
  • Shade: mix with black to darken.
  • Complement: 180 degrees across the wheel.
  • Analogous: within about 30 degrees on the wheel.

    • Why HSL helps

    Adjusting lightness/saturation in HSL space is more intuitive than editing RGB directly, so this tool generates steps in HSL.
