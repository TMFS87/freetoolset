📋 How to Use

  1. Set the minimum and maximum range.
  2. Choose how many numbers to generate.
  3. Toggle unique to avoid duplicates.
  4. Click Generate and copy the results.

❓ FAQ

Are the numbers truly random?

They use your browser’s crypto-grade random generator for strong unpredictability.

Can I get unique numbers only?

Yes — enable the unique option and the tool will not repeat a value within one draw.

What is the maximum range?

The range is limited only by JavaScript’s safe integer size, which is plenty for everyday use.
