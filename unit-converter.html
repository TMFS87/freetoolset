📐 Unit Converter

Convert between units of length, weight and temperature in one place. Fast, accurate and works entirely offline in your browser.

Enter a value to convert.
🔒 All processing happens in your browser. No data is uploaded to any server.

📋 How to Use

  1. Choose a category: length, weight or temperature.
  2. Enter a value and pick the "from" unit.
  3. Pick the "to" unit to convert.
  4. Use the swap button to reverse the conversion.

❓ FAQ

Which categories are supported?

Length, weight/mass and temperature. For more, try our dedicated Length, Weight, Speed, Area, Volume and Data converters.

How accurate are the conversions?

We use standard conversion factors and full floating-point precision, rounding only the displayed result.

Does it work offline?

Yes. All calculations run locally in your browser with no server calls.
