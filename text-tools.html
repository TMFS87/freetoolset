Frequently Asked Questions About Text Tools

How long a text can I process?
Most tools handle texts up to tens of thousands of characters. The Word Frequency Counter and Text Diff Checker are performance-optimized for large files.
Can it process Chinese text?
Yes. The Word Frequency Counter counts English words and Chinese characters separately, and the Chinese converter supports simplified/traditional switching and case conversion.
How do I save processed text?
You can copy the result directly, and some tools offer one-click download as a .txt file. All processing is done locally — no upload required.
How do these tools help with SEO?
The Word Frequency Counter helps analyze keyword density, while text sorting and deduplication help organize structured data — both are common aids for SEO professionals.
