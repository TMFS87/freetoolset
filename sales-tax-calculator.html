❓ FAQ

What is the difference between adding and removing tax?

Adding tax computes the gross price from a net amount. Removing tax extracts the net price and tax from a tax-inclusive (gross) amount.

Does this support VAT and GST?

Yes. VAT, GST and sales tax all work the same way — just enter the applicable rate.

How is remove-tax calculated?

Net = gross / (1 + rate/100), and tax = gross − net.
