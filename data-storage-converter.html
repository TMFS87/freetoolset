❓ FAQ

What is the difference between MB and MiB?

MB is decimal (1,000,000 bytes); MiB is binary (1,048,576 bytes). Drives are usually sold in MB/GB while OSes often report MiB/GiB.

How many bytes in a bit?

One byte equals 8 bits, so one bit is 0.125 bytes.

How many MB in a GB?

In decimal units, 1 GB = 1000 MB.
