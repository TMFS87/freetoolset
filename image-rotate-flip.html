🔁 Image Rotate & Flip

Rotate an image 90°, 180°, 270° or flip it horizontally / vertically. Runs entirely in your browser.

🔒 All processing happens in your browser. No data is uploaded to any server.

📋 How to Use

  1. Choose an image file.
  2. Click Rotate 90° / 180° / 270°.
  3. Or Flip Horizontal / Vertical.
  4. Download the corrected image.

❓ FAQ

Will this fix phone photos that look sideways?

Yes — rotate until the orientation is correct, then download. Note this does not read EXIF orientation automatically; you choose the angle.

Is the image uploaded?

No. All rotation is done locally with Canvas.

What format is the output?

PNG by default to preserve quality; you can choose JPG.

❓ More Questions

Can it fix a photo taken at an angle on my phone?

Yes. Rotate manually to the correct angle and download. Note this tool does not auto-read EXIF orientation; you choose the angle.

Are images uploaded?

No. All rotation is done locally on the Canvas.

📖 Deep Dive: Image Rotate & Flip

How it works

Use the Canvas rotate/scale transform to redraw the image onto a new canvas; after rotation the canvas dimensions swap accordingly (width and height swap at 90 or 270 degrees).

Tips

  • This tool does not auto-read EXIF; choose the angle manually.
  • Output defaults to PNG to preserve quality, with JPG optional.
  • Fully local processing; not uploaded.
